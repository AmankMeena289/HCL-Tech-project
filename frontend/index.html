        Definitions and rules behind these numbers
        Eligible encounter (rule E4)
        Completed, with a valid patient and hospital, discharged on or before 30 June 2026 23:59:59. Only these count toward revenue.
        Billed revenue
        Sum of billing-line charges (quantity × unit price, less discount) in INR, excluding tax.
        Quarantine
        Rows that break a cleansing rule (P1–P3 patients, S1–S2 services, E1–E4 encounters, L1–L4 billing lines) are kept with the reason instead of being deleted.
        Rerun safety
        Running the same batch again replaces that batch's rows, so nothing is counted twice.
